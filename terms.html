PurrCoach Terms of Use

Last updated: 2026-10-13

1. The service

PurrCoach is a consumer lifestyle app for logging cat care. It is informational and organizational only — not a veterinary or medical device.

2. Subscriptions

PurrCoach Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Price, duration, and trial terms are shown in the App Store checkout sheet. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple.

3. Acceptable use

Do not misuse the app to provide veterinary advice to others as if it were a professional diagnosis.

4. Disclaimer

Care insights are based on data you enter and may be incomplete. Always consult a licensed veterinarian for health concerns.
