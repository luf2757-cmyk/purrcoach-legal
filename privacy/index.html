PurrCoach Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

Not a veterinary service. PurrCoach helps you organize daily cat care. It does not diagnose or treat animals.

1. Data we store on device

By default, cat profiles and care logs (feeding, litter, medication, weight) stay on your device via local storage.

2. Anonymous identifier

We create an anonymous device ID in the Keychain to associate optional analytics and Apple subscriptions (via RevenueCat). You can view/copy it in Settings → Export my data.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to validate entitlements. We do not receive your full payment card details.

4. Optional product improvement

If you enable product improvement, we may send aggregated usage events (e.g. PostHog) and crash diagnostics (e.g. Sentry). You can turn this off in Settings.

5. Future cloud sync (Pro)

Household sharing / cloud sync, when launched, will require Sign in with Apple and will be described again in-app before you opt in.

6. Your choices

  • Disable notifications in iOS Settings
  • Disable product improvement in the app
  • Delete the app to remove local care logs
